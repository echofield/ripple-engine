Under the pre-2025 rules, their taxable capital gain would be €100,000 (Sale Price - Purchase Price). Under the new 2026 Finance Act rules, the base purchase value is reduced by the depreciation taken. Therefore, the base becomes €400,000, resulting in a taxable capital gain of €200,000.Strategic Consequence: This legislative shift absolutely obliterates the profitability of short-term "flip" or speculative strategies.
